57. Internet Connections
DSL stands for Digital Subscriber Line. With DSL connections, Internet users have faster download speeds that with a traditional phone line. The download speed is how long it takes to see a Web page or save a music file or an e-mail attachment. Another kind of DSL is ADSL. It stands for Asymmetric Digital Subscriber Line. Asymmetric means unequal. With ADSL, the upload speed is slower than the download speed. That means it takes longer to send information from a computer to the Internet that to receive information from the Internet. ADSL connections cost less than other DSL service. People usually have ADSL because they do not need to upload large amounts of information like a business would. To them it is more important to have fast download speeds.

60. Protecting Tropical Rainforests
(This is Shep O'Neal with the VOA Speical English Agriculture Report)
A major study has looked at efforts to save the tropical forests that remain in the world. Researchers found that less than five percent of the forest land is being protected. The study examined the use of sustainable management plans by forest operators. Such a plan seeks a continous flow of forest products, without damage to the future of the forest or its environment. The International Tropical Timber Organization did the study. The researchers studied the supervision of more than eight hundred million hectares of forests in thirty-three countries. This land represents two-thirds of all the natural tropical forests in the world.

The device was used to solve a problem for British storeowners. Many teenagers gathered in stores without buying anything. Storeowners used the MOsquito sound to make the teenagers leave the store. Some students downloaded the ring tone onto their cell phones and sent it to their friends. The ring tone quickly spread among teenagers in the United States and in other countries.

63. Saving Water
Water is important for us. We drink it every day. We use it to cook and wash things every day. We simple cannot live without water. But we have to be careful. Sometimes there is not enough fresh water. This has become a serious problem all over the world. In  the future, there may be a war over fresh water. So, we should not waste water. What should we do? Don't leave the sink running when you wash dishes or brush your teeth. Don't wash your car more than necessary. And immediately repair broken faucets and toilets. Leaking faucets and running toilets waste so much valuable water. These are just a few easy wasy to save water. There are hundreds more. Every day, we can do our part to conserver water. Try to save a little bit of water every day. That way, we can all have fresh water for years and years to come.

Finally, 1938 "Superman" was published in a comic book called Action Comics Number One. Superman was imediately popular. In less than a year, Superman became a newspaper comic strip. Mr.Siegel and Mr.Shuster did not make much money from their creation. They sold the legal rights to the comic for one hundred and thirty dollars. Still, the Superman story was very successful.
